# Favourite Bible Verses — Hebrew Background Layer

These verses will appear as faint Hebrew text in the background of the scripture intro. They are layered behind the main Exodus 31 verse at 2-5% opacity.

1. "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me" - Philippians 4:13

2. "Truly my soul finds rest in God; my salvation comes from Him. Truly He is my rock and my salvation; He is my fortress, I will never be shaken" - Psalm 62:1-2

3. "Jesus replied: 'Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.' This is the first and greatest commandment." - Matthew 22:37-38

4. "All Scripture is God-breathed and is useful for teaching, rebuking, correcting and training in righteousness, so that the servant of God may be thoroughly equipped for every good work." - 2 Timothy 3:16-17

5. "Through him all things were made; without him nothing was made that has been made" - John 1:3

6. "Therefore put on the full armour of God, so that when the day of evil comes, you may be able to stand your ground and after you have done everything, to stand." - Ephesians 6:13

7. "Do you not know that in a race all the runners run, but only one gets the prize? Run in such a way as to get the prize. Everyone who competes in the games goes into strict training. They do it to get a crown that will not last, but we do it to get a crown that will last forever." - 1 Corinthians 9:24-25

8. "Take my yoke upon you and learn from me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls. For my yoke is easy and my burden is light" - Matthew 11:29-30

---

## Usage

These will be displayed in Hebrew in the background of the scripture intro. The font file is `Hebrew_Font_for_Background.ttf`. They should repeat as a subtle horizontal texture, layered at 2-5% opacity behind the main Exodus 31 verse.

When adding new favourite verses to this list, simply append them here with the reference.
